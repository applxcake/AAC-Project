The Academic Scheduling Challenge

NP-Hard Combinatorial Optimization

In traditional university departments, creating a weekly timetable requires manually juggling hundreds of interdependent variables. Class cohorts cannot have overlapping sessions, instructors cannot be in two lecture halls simultaneously, specialized lab equipment is limited, and faculty workloads must stay within accredited limits without exhausting consecutive hours.

Manual preparation frequently causes classroom clashes, lab double-booking, and uneven teaching spreads that take weeks of administrative overhead to resolve.

How the AI Engine Works

Constraint Satisfaction Problem (CSP) Heuristics
PHASE 1: DOMAIN DEFINITION
Variable & Slot Modeling
Every required lecture and lab session is modeled with valid period domains, instructor allocations, and room compatibilities.
PHASE 2: HARD CONSTRAINTS
Forward Checking & MRV
Minimum Remaining Values (MRV) heuristic assigns high-constraint sessions first, enforcing zero overlaps for faculty, classes, and venues.
PHASE 3: VENUE MATCHING
Room-Type Compatibility
Laboratory courses are strictly assigned to laboratory spaces, while theory courses reserve standard lecture halls.
PHASE 4: SOFT OPTIMIZATION
Hill-Climbing Gap Reduction
Local stochastic search swaps non-conflicting slots to group lectures logically and eliminate awkward empty hours in faculty days.
